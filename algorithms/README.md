# Algorithms:Dimension Reduction and Clustering 
## Methods
In this section, two methods for reducing the dimensions of the 384-dimensional embeddings to 2 dimensions are utilized. Afterward, three clustering methods are applied to produce clusters using the resulting 2-dimensional embeddings.

Here are the methods applied:
* Dimension Reduction Methods:
    * tSNE
    * umap
* Clustering Methods:
    * KMeans
    * DBSCAN
    * HDBSCAN

By merging these two dimension reduction approaches with three clustering methods, a total of six clustering outcomes are generated.(See [outcomes](#outcomes))

After getting the clusters, each cluster should be labeled with a name.

### * Explanation of KMeans and Cluster Labels
A total of 38 topics have been identified by extracting information from journal titles(González-Márquez 2023). These topics serve as labels for categorizing each document based on its corresponding journal title. Consequently, during the training process of KMeans, the number of clusters is designated as 38.

The 38 topics are "neuroscience","cardiology","cancer","ecology","bioinformatics","chemistry","surgery","environment","material","microbiology","pediatric","immunology","psychology","psychiatry","genetics","nutrition","veterinary","engineering","education","physics","optics","nursing","neurology","radiology","ophthalmology","gynecology","rehabilitation","pathology","anesthesiology","dermatology","pharmacology","physiology","virology","biochemistry","computation","infectious","healthcare","ethics".

Given that all documents within a cluster can be associated with a particular topic, it becomes possible to compute the topic distribution for that cluster. The three most prominent topics are then combined to form the label for the cluster.

## Details of scripts
- `0-fetch_data_based_ids.ipynb` and `0-fetch_data_based_index.ipynb` are used to fetch data from remote machine to local machine. They are not necessary if data operated on the server. 
- `0-gen_tokens.py`: generating the index frameintell_pubmed_words.
- `1-pipeline-tSNE.ipynb`: generating 2d embeddings based on tSNE.
- `1.1-pipeline-tSNE-KMeans.ipynb`: generating clustering by combining tSNE and KMeans and storing the results into the index `frameintell_insights`. 
- `1.2-pipeline-tSNE-DBSCAN.ipynb`: generating clustering by combining tSNE and DBSCAN.
- `1.3-pipeline-tSNE-HDBSCAN.ipynb`: generating clustering by combining tSNE and HDBSCAN.
- `2-pipeline-UMAP.ipynb`: generate 2d embeddings based on umap.
- `2.1-pipeline-UMAP-KMeans.ipynb`: generating clustering by combining umap and KMeans.
- `2.2-pipeline-UMAP-DBSCAN.ipynb`: generating clustering by combining umap and DBSCAN.
- `2.3-pipeline-UMAP-HDBSCAN.ipynb`: generating clustering by combining umap and HDBSCAN.
- `3.1-gen_cluster_topics_based_journal_title.ipynb`: generating cluster topics by calculating the label distribution for journal title of doc
- `3.2-gen_cluster_topics_based_bert.ipynb`: generating cluster topics by bertTopic
